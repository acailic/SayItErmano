2) Curated catalog in `model_catalog.py`: `PARAKEET_CATALOG` entries for parakeet-tdt-0.6b-v2 (EN) and parakeet-tdt-0.6b-v3 (multilingual) pointing at real, directly-fetchable community ONNX exports — candidates to verify and pick from: `istupane/parakeet-tdt-0.6b-v2-onnx` (HF, has fp32/fp16/int8 + vocab), `csukuangfj/sherpa-onnx` GitHub release tarballs.
