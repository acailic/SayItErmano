Privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-11-21 · Seller: acailic (individual), Serbia

The short version

SayItErmano is a local application. Your recordings and transcripts never leave your computer. We do not collect, transmit, or sell your speech data, because no channel for it exists in the software.

What the application does

Speech-to-text runs entirely on your machine (Whisper models on your GPU or CPU). Transcripts and optional audio recordings are stored in ~/.local/state/sayit-ermano/ (audio retention is configurable, including automatic deletion after N days). Every network connection the application can make is documented and independently verifiable in the network egress audit:

  • Model download from Hugging Face — only until the model is cached locally
  • Update check against GitHub releases — can be disabled ([updates] check = false)
  • AI text polish — only if you explicitly configure an endpoint
  • Optional remote speech-to-text — only if you explicitly configure a server

No telemetry, analytics, crash reporting, or account system exists in the application.

What the website and store collect

This landing page is static and sets no cookies or trackers. Purchases are processed by Paddle.com as merchant of record — payment details, VAT handling, and receipts are governed by the Paddle privacy policy. We receive from Paddle only what is needed to deliver the product and support: your email address, purchase date, and country.

License keys

A supporter license key contains your email address and the purchase date, signed with Ed25519. It is verified offline, entirely on your machine. We keep a private record of issued keys (email + purchase date) for support and refund purposes, and delete it on request.
