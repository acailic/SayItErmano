Refund policy

Last updated: 2026-11-21 · Seller: acailic (individual), Serbia

14-day money-back guarantee

If the supporter license is not what you expected, email a refund request within 14 days of purchase and you will receive a full refund. No questions asked, no forms to fill beyond the request itself.

How to request a refund

  1. Reply to your Paddle order confirmation email, or
  2. Open an issue at github.com/acailic/SayItErmano/issues titled “Refund request” with the order number (the Paddle checkout email contains it).

Refunds are issued by Paddle.com (merchant of record) back to the original payment method. Processing usually takes 5–10 business days after approval; Paddle emails you a credit note for the VAT record. The seller-side steps live in the refund runbook.

After a refund

Your license key is deactivated for support purposes. The application itself keeps working — the free, open-source build is fully functional without a license, and no feature of the dictation pipeline is ever license-gated.

Chargebacks

Please contact us first — a refund is always cheaper and faster for you than a chargeback, and we never dispute refund requests made within the 14-day window.
